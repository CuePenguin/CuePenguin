Privacy Policy

CuePenguin is a private, unreleased software. No personal data, telemetry, or usage statistics are transmitted to external servers.

All project files, settings, and configurations remain 100% local on your executing system and local network environment.

Terms of Use

1. Private & Internal Availability

CuePenguin is a non-commercial, internal show-production platform built for in-house execution. Access is restricted to authorized team members and stage operators.

2. Zero Paywalls & Licensing

The platform is provided free of subscriptions, paywalls, or feature tiering. All tools are fully available out-of-the-box.

3. Workflow Attribution

CuePenguin is independently designed and developed. Inspired by LiveEdit workflows, but shares zero underlying code.
